Emma has been practicing in the '17 F-150 for half a year. She passed her test, has her learner's permit, and now Montana wants 50 supervised hours on the record.. 10 of them at night.. before she moves up.

The state's answer is a sheet of paper. Date, time, initials, repeat until 50.

That wasn't going to work in our house. So instead of the sheet, I wrote an app.

**[Drivetron](https://drivetron.app)**.

![Emma in the '17 F-150](/uploads/2026/11/drivetron-emma-f150.jpg)

## Start, Stop

Tap Start when you pull out, Stop when you're back. Any phone in the family can start a drive and any other can stop it, and everybody sees the same log.

Night minutes get split out from sunset and sunrise where you actually drove. Montana doesn't define "night" anywhere I could find, so sunset to sunrise it is.

Drives the teen logs on their own phone wait for a parent to confirm. Anything typed in after the fact, or with times edited, gets marked on the printout. I'm signing a state form that says these hours happened, so the log should be honest about which ones were timed live.

When the hours are done, it prints a log laid out like Montana's sheet. Sign it and bring it along.

![Drivetron](/uploads/2026/11/drivetron-landing.png)

## Then Every Other State

Montana was supposed to be it. Then I looked at the other states. Nobody agrees on anything.

- **California** starts night 30 minutes after sunset. **Maryland** starts it 30 minutes *before*.
- **Texas** only counts 2 hours a day. **North Carolina** caps it at 10 a week.
- **Pennsylvania** wants 5 hours in bad weather. **Alaska** wants 10 "in challenging circumstances."
- **New Hampshire** doesn't have a learner's permit at all.
- **Arkansas** and **Hawaii** don't require any hours. Plenty of sites say Hawaii wants 50.. I couldn't find that anywhere official.

Every number points back to a DMV page, a statute, or the form a parent signs. Each state got its own page, plus a [comparison of all 50 and D.C.](https://drivetron.app/driving-hours-by-state)

![Supervised driving hours by state](/uploads/2026/11/drivetron-hours-by-state.png)

If the sheet does work in your house, there's a free [printable log for every state](https://drivetron.app/montana/printable-log).
