Emma has been practicing in the '17 F-150 for half a year. She passed her test, has her learner's permit, and now Montana wants 50 supervised hours on the record.. 10 of them at night.. before she moves up.

The state's answer is a sheet of paper. Date, time, initials, repeat until 50.

That wasn't going to work in our house. So instead of the sheet, I wrote an app: [Drivetron](https://drivetron.app).

![Emma in the '17 F-150](/uploads/2026/11/drivetron-emma-f150.jpg)

## Start, Stop

Tap Start when you pull out, Stop when you're back. Katie can start a drive on her phone and I can stop it on mine, and we all see the same log.

![Emma's log, 26 minutes into a drive with Katie](/uploads/2026/11/drivetron-emma-log.png)

Bottom of that screen is the app calling us out. An hour and a half in, at the pace we're going, Emma finishes her hours after the permit expires. Pick up the pace. Fair.

Night minutes get split out from sunset and sunrise where you actually drove. Montana doesn't define "night" anywhere I could find, so sunset to sunrise it is.

Drives Emma logs on her own phone wait for one of us to confirm. Anything typed in after the fact, or with the times edited, gets marked on the printout. I'm signing a state form that says these hours happened, so the log should be honest about which ones were timed live.

When she's done, it prints a log laid out like Montana's sheet. Sign it and bring it along.

## Then Every Other State

Montana was supposed to be it. Then I looked at the other states. Nobody agrees on anything.

- **California** starts night 30 minutes after sunset. **Maryland** starts it 30 minutes *before*.
- **Texas** only counts 2 hours a day. **North Carolina** caps it at 10 a week.
- **Pennsylvania** wants 5 hours in bad weather. **Alaska** wants 10 "in challenging circumstances."
- **New Hampshire** doesn't have a learner's permit at all.
- **Arkansas** and **Hawaii** don't require any hours. Plenty of sites say Hawaii wants 50.. I couldn't find that anywhere official.

Every number points back to a DMV page, a statute, or the form a parent signs. [All 50 and D.C. side by side](https://drivetron.app/driving-hours-by-state), if you're curious how weird it gets.

![Supervised driving hours by state](/uploads/2026/11/drivetron-hours-by-state.png)

If the sheet does work in your house, there's a free [printable one for every state](https://drivetron.app/montana/printable-log).
