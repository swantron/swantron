The girls are back in Glasgow with my parents for the week. Time for another relaxing aggressive hike.

Katie and I went back up [Baldy](https://outsidebozeman.com/trails-tours/hiking-trails/m-mt-baldy-trail) — published as 8 miles round trip, 4,300 feet of gain — this time with a bit more tact.

![Katie on the trail through wildflowers](/uploads/2026/07/mt-baldy-meadow.jpg)

![summit selfie with Bozeman below](/uploads/2026/07/mt-baldy-summit-again.jpg)

Kept going past the trail check-in further down the ridge. Looking back:

![looking back down the ridge past the check-in](/uploads/2026/07/mt-baldy-ridge.jpg)

Peloton tried to keep up. Not accurate, but it logged 13.88 miles, 5,297 feet of gain, and a little over five hours. GPS on a ridge does what GPS on a ridge does.

Fun trip.. looking forward to the next one.
