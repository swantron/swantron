[Saturday](/2026/08/15/utvtron-belt/) the nine-year-old belt came apart on the last hill into Four Corners. I pulled the fiber out of the outlet duct and drove it home. Then I put a new one in.

This ride wasn't science. It was break-in. Tips in the right places so the card isn't lying about which hose is which.

## The Swap

Cover off. Old fluff still clinging to the inner housing lip. New belt on the Polaris primary and the TEAM secondary. Blue paint on the primary nut so I know I actually torqued it.

![New CVT belt on the Polaris primary and TEAM secondary, cover off](/uploads/2026/08/utvtron-aug20-belt-new.png)

![Inner clutch housing after cleanup.. ducting, loom, leftover belt fiber on the seal lip](/uploads/2026/08/utvtron-aug20-housing.png)

Logged the swap in [wrenchtron](https://wrenchtron.com) as belt replacement. Same Google account utvtron signs into now, so the garage and the maintenance log are one machine again.

## The Tips

ThermoPro in the cargo recess behind the seats. Three tips live before we rolled.. 88 / 89 / 87. Jack 4 empty. Names on the ride: **Engine bay / Clutch / Ambient**. Clutch tip in the *outlet* duct this time.. the hose that leaves the housing, not the intake I mislabeled back in [August](/2026/08/10/utvtron-duct/).

![ThermoPro TP25 in the RZR cargo recess, three tips live before the break-in ride](/uploads/2026/08/utvtron-aug20-thermopro.png)

## The Ride

Eighteen minutes. 7.4 miles. Eight impacts. Easy loop.. not River Road to Amsterdam, not 65 up Cottonwood. Clutch outlet peaked **130°**. Bay 104°. Ambient 91°. Chart climbs, then sits. Last five minutes average ~127° on the clutch channel. Boring. Good.

![utvtron share card: New belt ride, Clutch 130°F, 18 min, 7.4 mi](/uploads/2026/08/utvtron-aug20-share.png)

CSV: 671 rows. One hole at ~9 minutes.. about 99 seconds with no samples. The app dropped; I hit Resume. Session held together as one ride. Probe needed a reconnect after the kill. The card is still honest.

Saturday's shred peaked **164°** on that same outlet channel after a hard hill. Tonight was half an hour of not doing that. Different question.

## What This Proves

The new belt will take a normal evening loop without cooking. Outlet air on a fresh belt looks like warm duct air, not a funeral. Tips match the plugs. The logger still isn't a crystal ball.. and tonight it didn't need to be.

More miles before anything spicy. Live: [utvtron.vercel.app](https://utvtron.vercel.app)
